# Introduction

Welcome to **Keep Track** — a personal budgeting app built for individuals who want to be intentional about their money.

## What is Keep Track?

Keep Track is a monthly budgeting system for Android, iOS, Windows, and macOS. It helps you plan your income and expenses before the month begins, track actuals as they happen, save with purpose, and stay on top of everything you owe or are owed.

It is not a business tool. There are no invoices, no clients, no accounting. Keep Track is built for one person managing their own financial life.

## Core Features

### Monthly Budgets
Create income and expense budget groups for each month. Add categories (Salary, Rent, Food, Transport) with planned amounts. As you log transactions, actuals fill in against your plan so you always know where you stand.

### Savings Wallets
Named balance buckets that work like envelopes — Emergency Fund, Travel, New Laptop. Deposit and withdraw freely, with a full transaction history per wallet.

### Goals
Set a target amount and a deadline. Track monthly contributions and watch your progress toward bigger purchases or milestones.

### Debts & Receivables
Log money you owe (car loan, personal debt) and money owed to you (friend who borrowed cash). Record partial payments and track the remaining balance over time.

### Subscriptions
A dedicated list of all your recurring charges — streaming, software, gym — with next billing dates and monthly cost equivalents.

### Planned Payments
Recurring bills with a frequency (monthly, quarterly, yearly). Electricity, internet, insurance — schedule them so they never catch you by surprise.

### Budget Profiles
Run multiple budget plans independently. Useful for keeping personal finances and freelance or side-income budgets in separate spaces.

### Transaction Planner
Schedule expected future transactions — an incoming invoice, a one-off payment — so you can plan cash flow ahead of time.

| Platform | Minimum Version |
|----------|-----------------|

| iOS      | 14.0            |
| Windows  | 10 (64-bit)     |
| macOS    | 12 Monterey     |

## Tech Stack

Keep Track is built with Flutter (frontend), NestJS (backend), and MongoDB (database). Data syncs to the cloud when online and works offline via local cache.
