### iOS
- iOS 14.0 or later
- iPhone 6s or newer
- 200 MB available storage

### Windows
- Windows 10 (64-bit) or later
- 4 GB RAM minimum
- 500 MB available storage

### macOS
- macOS 12 Monterey or later
- Apple Silicon or Intel Mac
- 500 MB available storage

## Installation

### Android (.apk)
1. Download the `.apk` file above
2. On your device, go to **Settings → Apps → Install unknown apps** and allow your browser
3. Tap the downloaded file and follow the prompts
4. Launch **Keep Track** from your home screen

### iOS
Coming to the App Store — stay tuned.

### Windows (.exe)
1. Download the `.exe` installer above
2. Run the file — if Windows SmartScreen appears, click **More info → Run anyway**
3. Follow the setup wizard
4. Launch **Keep Track** from the Start Menu or desktop shortcut

### macOS (.dmg)
1. Download the `.dmg` file above
2. Open the disk image and drag **Keep Track** to your Applications folder
3. On first launch, right-click the app and choose **Open** to bypass Gatekeeper
4. Sign in or create your account

## First Time Setup

1. **Create an account** — sign up with your email or continue with Google
2. **Set your currency** — go to Settings → Currency and pick yours
3. **Create your first budget** — head to Budget, tap the month, and start adding groups
4. **Add your savings wallets** — open the Savings tab and create buckets for your goals
5. **Log your first transaction** — tap the + button anywhere in the app

Need help? Check the [Quick Start guide](/docs/quickstart).
